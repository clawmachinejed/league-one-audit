## Proof classes and carry-forward rules

- **Source/offline proof:** types, deterministic fixtures, lint/build, browser compatibility and scope checks establish only their asserted source behavior. Mocks and authored SQL do not establish installed database semantics.
- **Actual restricted SQL:** the existing guarded harness must prove real installed schema/permissions, exact selected assertions and committed readback under genuine restricted roles. Bind run/profile/module hashes, PostgreSQL version, selected/collected/filtered counts, hooks, retries/skips, immutable journals and acknowledged cleanup to the exact reviewed SHA.
- **Bounded live acquisition:** one authorized public username → complete observed 2026 list → actual isolated storage → stored-only reader establishes that sampled connection path. Keep its discovery/capture bounds and raw-versus-sanitized evidence distinction; unchanged refreshes do not prove controlled corrections or all formats.
- **Elapsed operations and restore:** sustained load, actual crash/restart, independent worker contention, outage recovery, reader/storage measurements and restore require their own real proof. Injected exceptions, timestamp aging, successful cleanup, configuration values and historical live passes cannot substitute.

Before carrying a prior assertion forward, record its original SHA/run/hash, exact covered behavior, proof class and current dependency/file mapping. Carry-forward is a reviewed relevance claim, **not a rerun**. Missing local artifacts remain historical references; do not invent a fresh hash match. Changes to the relevant adapter, normalizer, schema/function/grants, acceptance/reader, scheduling/fence, fixture/oracle/profile/reporter/harness or dependencies invalidate unreviewed transfer of affected assertions. Identify the impacted proof and bind new qualification to the final immutable candidate. Preserve historical failures and successful evidence regardless of later invalidation.

A source-only documentation change can preserve unchanged executable evidence while receiving its own source CI/preview record. Required final-candidate SQL/operating assertions still need actual authorization and source-bound execution; an old ledger total or a preserved branch cannot satisfy them. Keep local completion, publication, preview, merge, database installation, deployment and activation separate.
