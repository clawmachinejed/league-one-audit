## Bundle exit gates and implementation order

1. **B1 exact-period matchup.** Establish mapped native period and participant identity, exact lineup/bench/empty slots, official team/player scores and adjustment evidence, and evidence-labeled state. Also qualify current starter/reserve/taxi groups separately from already accepted held players. Use the existing administration network capture and one writer. Keep current player metadata, NFL game state, forecasts and probabilities as separately scoped references. Retain old observations and hashes. Compare old/new from one capture for ordinary, unpaired, zero, corrected, partial, stale, retry and rollover examples. A migration must be additive; qualify deterministic, restartable retained-evidence processing with mapping/period/population fences before claiming migration readiness. Actual production replay is separately authorized. Old callers keep v1 behavior. Application rollback stops shadow acceptance and leaves immutable receipts/history. No production backfill is authorized here.
2. **B2 season overview.** Build on B1 team and period identities. Qualify schedules, official record/PF/PA and source rank versus local order. Join existing projection and metric references without changing their workers. Compare historical and current contexts, corrections, incomplete weeks and rollover. Retained evidence and resumable replay require exact season/mapping checks; old readers and rollback remain intact.
3. **B3 transactions/activity.** Preserve status, participants, player/pick/FAAB movements and actual waiver detail with window/pagination completeness. Qualify present, missing and unsupported source fields independently. Reuse the existing weekly collector/writer, compare profile and league presentations from the same retained capture, and fence replay by source revision/season/week.
4. **B4 historical continuity.** Verify annual chain and attribution, completed official results and curated facts separately. Process retained bounded evidence, never infer a predecessor from names. Compare existing manager history and warnings. Keep old readers and immutable source history during rollback.
5. **Cross-resource readiness.** Every ledger row has a typed implementation/reuse path and fixture with normal, missing and unsupported evidence. Mapped identities, periods and source times agree across B1–B4. Existing IDs, immutable history, scoring profiles, frozen baselines, snapshot publication and official values remain unchanged. Same-capture comparisons have no unexplained official-value differences. Required retained-data processing is deterministic, resumable and idempotent after interruption; migration and application rollback paths are tested without deleting history or widening grants. Exact-SHA disposable SQL, independent review and actual Preview evidence are recorded. Real authorized second-provider samples are reconciled or the qualification remains explicitly open. Before Step 3, identify a populated pilot cohort with complete accepted data or a qualified compatibility path, and record the exact reader switch, compare window, monitoring and rollback procedure. Step 3 reader cutover, production replay/backfill, provider enrollment and Step 5 fleet capacity each require their own scope/release decision.
