## Minimum stored contract

Use an immutable typed qualification supplement keyed by the existing roster capture receipt. Reuse its attempt ordering, mapping, raw content, acquisition witness, original fence and observation clocks. Do not create another GET, queue, attempt, receipt or resource head for standings. Do not widen the existing players or managers acceptance policies. A new immutable per-receipt marker records structural qualification/capacity, population and field completeness; typed team rows carry canonical season-team identity and the nine native fields above.

`official-standings.ts` exports `projectOfficialStandings(normalized)`, version `sleeper-official-standings-v1`, and the direct reader contract. The parser uses the raw roster envelope even when unrelated players/ownership cause the legacy normalizer to reject. Numeric positive safe-integer roster IDs are normalized to strings consistently with the existing roster normalizer. Duplicate, malformed or foreign-league identities reject the projection; field-level missing/null/invalid states do not erase other valid fields. Limits are twenty observed rows and one MiB of typed projection, reported as capacity rather than truncation. The writer remains responsible for the original envelope, source identity, mapping and witness validation.

The supplement preserves `SeasonFact` (`known/absent/null/invalid`) and `SeasonPointsFact` component/value/policy semantics. `season-overview-fields.ts` extracts the existing exact decimal/field helpers without changing the legacy season-overview outputs, accepted-player requirement or presenter. It performs no standings reconstruction or sorting. Parsed-number decimal precision is retained; precision already lost before JSON parsing cannot be recovered.

Population and record completeness are distinct. The reader exposes `entitySet: full/partial/unknown`, `recordCompleteness: complete/partial/unknown`, and `population: proven/unproved/mismatch`. An absent same-acquisition population document permits independently valid supplied fields with unproved entity coverage. A count mismatch within otherwise valid same-acquisition population evidence permits only partial entity coverage. A foreign, contradictory-scope or mismatched acquisition witness remains an identity failure and must not be downgraded to unknown. Empty roster arrays contain zero observed teams; they do not prove an empty league against a positive or unknown expected population. Invalid/capacity markers remain unavailable with explicit reasons.

Native team-count qualification reads the original receipt's independently linked complete league capture, observation/hash, source identity and acquisition evidence. It can establish `total_rosters` even when the legacy receipt's expected count was cleared by a scoring-compatibility gate. The standings supplement and reader independently validate that fresh native evidence; they do not overwrite the legacy expected count, widen player/manager acceptance, or borrow an older population proof.

Current direct reads select the latest roster attempt **before** joining/filtering receipts and qualification. Request reads select the latest core dispatch for that request and league **before** filtering. A newer failure or missing marker must remain visible; neither read silently selects an older good qualification. An explicitly requested old receipt remains readable against its valid current mapping with its original age. Pre-CP13 receipts without a supplement return `typed_standings_not_recorded`; the reader does not backfill on read. Other requests' newer attempts must not invalidate a correctly bound historical request capture.

`composeOfficialResults({mapping, standings, periods, brackets})` reuses stored exact-score/context and bracket reads. Each component retains its own receipt and observation time. Current season standings remain season-to-date and are not relabeled as past-period standings. Weekly outcome, finality and series membership are explicitly unknown until an evidence-backed interpretation exists. Supplied bracket `w/l` is returned with bracket-family/match identity and source receipt; finality remains separate. No aggregate, score comparator, new provisional calculator, ranking policy or cross-namespace join is introduced. The composed result claims only selected-resource scope.

Public intake/refresh exposure is opt-in through `officialResultsVersion: 'v1'`. Its standings resource is bound to the original dispatch even if core completion fails. Selected periods reuse CP9/10 and brackets reuse CP11/12. Omitted options preserve the legacy reader shape and behavior. A resource becoming readable must not mark incomplete roster, manager, bracket-pair or request work complete.

The author/root and independent upstream/reader/contract reviewers agreed this supplement is smaller than a separate standings acceptance lifecycle while preserving independent field coverage. Read-only projection unchanged would remain player-gated and lack independent durable typing; weakening player acceptance would corrupt its meaning. A separate generic lifecycle is not justified while the existing receipt, attempt and witness can carry the result.

At this design baseline, local pure tests covered retained zero-field parsing, synthetic field states/identity/capacity, unrelated-player rejection independence, corrections, exact decimals, namespace isolation and unknown interpretation. Hosted restricted-LOGIN qualification, late-COMMIT and ordinary failure/recovery were then pending, and no new live capture had been performed. Their subsequent bounded qualification and the October 12 public inventory are recorded in the current overlay above; source tests and local diagnostics do not replace hosted evidence.

CP13 retains the obligation to establish supported official result/standings semantics and representative ties/byes/multiweek completion/correction. Unknown output safely represents present uncertainty but does not close that obligation. CP20 owns complete within-2026 periods/legs/history composition and application of established semantics; CP23–26 own fleet refresh and sustained freshness proof, and CP30 owns coherent final-source qualification. This increment does not prove the frozen few-minute active-data target or the fifteen-minute results/standings refresh target.
