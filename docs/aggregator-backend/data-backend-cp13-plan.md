## Outcome and scope

A stored backend reader returns the official season facts and supplied competition outcomes that the retained Sleeper evidence supports, with observation time, field presence, exact resource identity, and corrections. It distinguishes provider-supplied facts, unknown outcomes, and any existing locally derived order; no new provisional result calculator is required. A reader must not manufacture an official win, tie, bye, rank, playoff-series aggregate, champion, or finality from scores, calendar position, array order, or current roster totals.

1. **Data resource:** 2026 season-to-date standings facts from the existing roster acquisition; exact native-period scores and group identities from CP10; supplied bracket outcomes and relationships from CP11-12; applicable settings/context from CP9. Public Sleeper usernames remain eligible without a named-league whitelist. Head-to-head redraft, keeper and dynasty fixtures are required; special structures limit only the affected resource or conclusion.
2. **Existing path:** existing `lib/sleeper.ts` acquisition and `league-administration` acceptance/receipt owners feed typed SQL and stored-only readers. Reuse field and decimal semantics from `aggregator/season-overview-source.ts` and `season-overview-standings.ts`, and reuse exact-matchup and bracket readers; extend ordinary intake/refresh read composition. The existing season-overview reader itself requires accepted players coverage and cannot be the independent standings reader unchanged. No new provider feed, worker, scheduler, page, projection calculation, account workflow, or production selection.
3. **Persisted result:** independently validated, receipt-bound, queryable standings facts and their field states, exact captured native values, canonical season/team identities, immutable observation/version history, and a bounded composed result response whose components retain their own receipt and source time. Existing accepted membership, score and bracket resources keep their independent semantics.
4. **Real evidence and gaps (planning-date baseline):** this document records requirements, not executed CP13 assertions. The implementation and local diagnostics now appear in the [current evidence record](data-backend-evidence.md); the following pending statements describe the original planning baseline. CP10 score parity and CP11-12 bracket qualification are prerequisites with their original source/run limits. Dedicated typed standings persistence, CP13 result composition, latest-source restricted-role SQL and combined correction readback are still pending. Paid SQL, provisioning, migrations, credentials, merge, deployment and activation need their applicable separate authority.

Resolve these gates before choosing the implementation:

1. **Evidence and meaning.** Create a fact-to-native-field/endpoint/retained-evidence inventory for records, exact totals, supplied rank/seed, outcomes, finality, ties, byes and multiweek relationships. Record whether each is observed, absent from an inspected capture, uncollected, uninterpreted, contradicted or established unavailable from the supported source. [Sleeper's official API documentation](https://docs.sleeper.com/) identifies roster record/score components, weekly scores and bracket outcome fields, but its omission of a finality flag does not establish that all relevant source evidence is unavailable. Do not invent positive provider evidence or use unknown to close an unimplemented obtainable obligation.
2. **Completeness and selection.** Specify required versus optional fields; authoritative absence/removal versus invalid/incomplete observations; when a new capture changes the selected current facts; and exact historical/remap behavior. A greatest-qualified-receipt query silently implements last-good fallback unless explicitly represented. Newer pending/failed work, retained history and current completeness must remain distinguishable. Pre-CP13 receipts have no typed qualification by implication; choose an explicit old-receipt policy.
3. **Population and canonical identity.** Registration alone does not create teams. Existing roster team creation depends on accepted content; manager fallback depends on valid manager coverage. Independently validate native team IDs, duplicates, league/season mapping and population, and reuse the existing canonical identity owner without inventing player or manager acceptance. Distinguish scoring-profile incompatibility from failed league-population acquisition: the current R047 witness guard requires same-acquisition population evidence, and a league write exception can stop the core loop before roster persistence. Neither storage alternative alone removes that prerequisite. Define safe partial retention, discoverability and coverage when population is unproved; any required change to `runtime.ts`, the public core coordinator or SQL witness validation needs specific independent review without weakening source identity or fencing.
4. **Failed-core discoverability.** `public-intake.ts` and its SQL completion function require accepted settings, players and managers before candidate receipt IDs are checkpointed. A durable standings row is insufficient if that request cannot read it. Prove immutable request/dispatch-to-capture binding or a separately validated partial evidence checkpoint while core remains incomplete. A latest-for-current-mapping read is a distinct explicitly labeled contract, not proof of that request's completion. Recovery currently examines request revisions; prove it preserves and reuses the partial evidence safely.
5. **Replay, time and transaction safety.** Preserve predecessor input hashes by excluding new extension fields before delegation as existing extensions do. Do not silently backfill historical attempts. Equal-content network capture may reuse a legacy observation ID, so source age comes from the new capture receipt. Each fresh receipt needs its own qualification/fence enforcement at delayed COMMIT, including equal content; a trigger only on first typed-content insertion is insufficient. Expected optional-field invalidity is a field/coverage result; integrity, forged projection, mapping and expired-fence failures still roll back the affected atomic write. Do not promise independence from arbitrary database corruption or failed integrity checks.
6. **Downstream compatibility and cost.** Preserve separately timed score/settings/standings/bracket components; do not turn current-display Bundle Two into an atomic historical snapshot. Missing users already allows official standings while suppressing name-dependent local ordering. Preserve legacy display defaults/rounding/order in `lib/transform.ts`, production `getStandings`, DATA enrollment, account activation, profile creation and projection publication. Reads remain stored-only. Both candidate designs add CPU/SQL/locking work under the original 20-second fence; no extra GET does not prove deadline compliance. Measure changed/equal captures, relation blocking and delayed COMMIT, with explicit row/byte/read limits.

The smaller supplement is a provisional architectural preference, not an accepted runtime design or a claim that it is already safest or fastest. If it fails these gates, use the existing generic acceptance owner with a concrete explanation of the extra lifecycle requirement. Do not build a parallel custom worker, source feed, identity system or standings head algorithm.

## Meaning required at the reader boundary

- **Standings observations:** retain native W/L/T and PF/PA components and supplied rank/seed independently. Missing, null, invalid and valid zero remain distinct. Rank/seed absence does not invalidate representable records or scores. Existing whole-plus-hundredths normalization retains component evidence; it does not recover precision lost before parsed JSON.
- **Time:** season totals describe the retained season-to-date observation. A capture made in week 10 is not week 3's standings because the caller selected week 3. Read time, exact replay and cache hits do not create a fresh source observation. A genuinely new successful network capture with unchanged content may advance source time on its own new receipt/provenance, without rewriting old receipt times or pretending the content changed. A late provider correction can produce a new version while old receipt reads remain stable.
- **Separate status dimensions:** field presence, official/derived authority, publication or outcome state, finality, coverage and freshness must be independently represented. A supplied bracket winner can be official while finality remains unknown. No invented positive provider-finality fixture may stand in for actual retained source evidence.
- **Results:** source-supplied bracket winner/loser is official evidence only within that bracket match's source scope. Its presence does not establish league-wide completion, a regular-season result, a mapped weekly result, or championship finality. Provider league status remains a league-level observation. Existing derived comparisons remain separate from official-result fields; adding a new provisional comparison calculator is outside this minimum increment.
- **Ties and byes:** equal totals do not establish an official tie. One entrant, a null opponent or an unpaired weekly row does not alone establish a bye. Preserve supplied source structure and explain unknown result meaning.
- **Multiweek playoffs:** retain independent legs and supplied bracket outcomes. Produce a series link, aggregate or final result only when its identity, included legs, applicable rules and outcome authority are evidenced. The negative case (no link/finality evidence) must pass explicitly; a fabricated positive fixture is not proof that Sleeper supplies that evidence in practice. CP13 owns supported result interpretation and representative multiweek completion/correction cases; CP20 owns applying these semantics across the complete current-season history.
- **Derivation:** supplied provider rank/seed remains separate from any current `compareTeams` presentation order. Unsupported tie-break/scoring analytics do not suppress reliable official fields. CP13 introduces no new standings reconstruction or projection engine.
- **Partial availability:** each component can remain useful without all other families. A pending latest bracket pair and a prior complete pair must be distinguishable if both are exposed; do not silently blend collection cycles. No automatic stale fallback is claimed until its reader contract and source-age behavior are tested.
- **Stored-only access:** successful and failed reads perform no provider calls. Every returned fact identifies the provider, league/season/team or bracket/native-period namespace, receipt/version, source time and relevant applicability/coverage limitation.
