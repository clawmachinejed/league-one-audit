## Outcome and scope

A stored backend reader returns the official season facts and supplied competition outcomes that the retained Sleeper evidence supports, with observation time, field presence, exact resource identity, and corrections. It distinguishes provider-supplied facts, provisional comparisons, unknown outcomes, and any existing locally derived order. A reader must not manufacture an official win, tie, bye, rank, playoff-series aggregate, champion, or finality from scores, calendar position, array order, or current roster totals.

1. **Data resource:** 2026 season-to-date standings facts from the existing roster acquisition; exact native-period scores and group identities from CP10; supplied bracket outcomes and relationships from CP11-12; applicable settings/context from CP9. Public Sleeper usernames remain eligible without a named-league whitelist. Head-to-head redraft, keeper and dynasty fixtures are required; special structures limit only the affected resource or conclusion.
2. **Existing path:** existing `lib/sleeper.ts` acquisition and `league-administration` acceptance/receipt owners feed typed SQL and stored-only readers. Reuse `aggregator/season-overview-source.ts`, `season-overview-standings.ts`, exact-matchup and bracket readers; extend ordinary intake/refresh read composition. No new provider feed, worker, scheduler, page, projection calculation, account workflow, or production selection.
3. **Persisted result:** independently accepted, receipt-bound, queryable standings facts and their field states, exact captured native values, canonical season/team identities, immutable observation/version history, and a bounded composed result response whose components retain their own receipt and source time. Existing accepted membership, score and bracket resources keep their independent semantics.
4. **Real evidence and gaps:** this document records requirements, not executed CP13 assertions. CP10 score parity and CP11-12 bracket qualification are prerequisites with their original source/run limits. Dedicated typed standings persistence, CP13 result composition, latest-source restricted-role SQL and combined correction readback are still pending. Paid SQL, provisioning, migrations, credentials, merge, deployment and activation need their applicable separate authority.

## Meaning required at the reader boundary

- **Standings observations:** retain native W/L/T and PF/PA components and supplied rank/seed independently. Missing, null, invalid and valid zero remain distinct. Rank/seed absence does not invalidate representable records or scores. Existing whole-plus-hundredths normalization retains component evidence; it does not recover precision lost before parsed JSON.
- **Time:** season totals describe the retained season-to-date observation. A capture made in week 10 is not week 3's standings because the caller selected week 3. Read time, exact replay and cache hits do not create a fresh source observation. A genuinely new successful network capture with unchanged content may advance source time on its own new receipt/provenance, without rewriting old receipt times or pretending the content changed. A late provider correction can produce a new version while old receipt reads remain stable.
- **Separate status dimensions:** field presence, official/derived authority, publication or outcome state, finality, coverage and freshness must be independently represented. A supplied bracket winner can be official while finality remains unknown. No invented positive provider-finality fixture may stand in for actual retained source evidence.
- **Results:** source-supplied bracket winner/loser is official evidence only within that bracket match's source scope. Its presence does not establish league-wide completion, a regular-season result, a mapped weekly result, or championship finality. Provider league status remains a league-level observation. If a provisional score comparison is exposed, it is separately derived and cannot populate an official-result field.
- **Ties and byes:** equal totals do not establish an official tie. One entrant, a null opponent or an unpaired weekly row does not alone establish a bye. Preserve supplied source structure and explain unknown result meaning.
- **Multiweek playoffs:** retain independent legs and supplied bracket outcomes. Produce a series link, aggregate or final result only when its identity, included legs, applicable rules and outcome authority are evidenced. The negative case (no link/finality evidence) must pass explicitly; a fabricated positive fixture is not proof that Sleeper supplies that evidence in practice.
- **Derivation:** supplied provider rank/seed remains separate from any current `compareTeams` presentation order. Unsupported tie-break/scoring analytics do not suppress reliable official fields. CP13 introduces no new standings reconstruction or projection engine.
- **Partial availability:** each component can remain useful without all other families. A pending latest bracket pair and a prior complete pair must be distinguishable if both are exposed; do not silently blend collection cycles. No automatic stale fallback is claimed until its reader contract and source-age behavior are tested.
- **Stored-only access:** successful and failed reads perform no provider calls. Every returned fact identifies the provider, league/season/team or bracket/native-period namespace, receipt/version, source time and relevant applicability/coverage limitation.
