## Capacity accounting rules

The combined DATA-core rate replaces overlapping historical DATA core, proof, link, standings and raw-observation terms. Synthetic controls remain separate, and ordinary controls stay in the unpriced U. Standalone A and B1 savings are never added together. Logical tuple and proof-column sizes are diagnostic measures; they are not added to physical allocation or treated as equal to reclaimable bytes. Warmed sequential page growth can differ between batches. The fixture also creates one fresh source-manager account per core; its league_source_manager_accounts allocation is classified shared DATA and remains in the measured DATA total. An ordinary repeat for an existing target need not create that row. Its per-table attribution is disclosed, with no invented subtraction or savings. The observed mean is a finite fixture sensitivity, not an established recurring production rate.

The conditional horizon is January 1, 2026 through April 1, 2028 exclusive: 821 days, including an assumed 126 active days at 24 hours/day and 695 inactive days. Five-minute sensitivity gives 591,728 core events over sixteen leagues. The actual scheduler sets core due_at to observed_at plus four minutes; that separate sensitivity gives 736,880 events. Freshness is a target, not proof of either observed polling cadence. The measured core includes all four typed resource captures; the forecast gives no unsupported credit for a slower settings cadence.

The all-family ledger distinguishes complete inventory from complete rates and qualified capacity. Event volumes remain explicit cadence/period assumptions; unmeasured byte rates are null, and additional source inventory contributes an explicit unknown U. A priced subtotal below 10 GiB is not a pass: the complete condition is pricedSubtotal + explicitlyAssumedBytes + U <= budget (explicitlyAssumedBytes is zero in the default inputs). The once-only global catalog is charged once; recurring catalog attempts and changed whole versions remain separate. Prior assumptions such as seventeen earlier periods, one twenty-pick draft or one result report are not observed upper bounds. This fixture's eighty held players per league also cannot be blindly scaled into the earlier hosted 160-held-player workload.


The ledger covers the eight official contract families through separately named workload events. Generic receipt/attempt/acceptance/raw/control tables are shared by families, so costs are attributed by disjoint event contributions, not by charging the same table once per family.

It prepares sessions/PIDs before the original four-second fence and records bounded predicate/timing/error diagnostics. The fence is not reset or extended after reservation. A second attempt was manually reviewed only after the repaired source passed 186 profile tests, TypeScript, changed-file lint and independent fixture/harness review. The original thirty-minute campaign clock and cumulative 512-reservation limit remain in force; there is no automatic retry.
